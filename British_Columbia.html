ROAD TRIP • ITINERARY • BRITISH COLUMBIA

The Ultimate 6-Day British Columbia Travel Guide

Waterfalls, turquoise lakes, mountain highways, forest trails and coastal sunsets — this is the exact 6-day route we followed through British Columbia.

6 DAYS ROAD TRIP SCENIC + OUTDOORS
BRITISH COLUMBIA Collect moments, not things. — F & K
TRIP LENGTH 6 Days
STARTING POINT Vancouver
BEST WAY Road Trip
TRIP STYLE Nature + Hiking
BIGGEST TIP Start Early
IMPORTANT BOOKING Joffre Lakes Permit

From Fraser Valley and Harrison Hot Springs to the Sea to Sky Highway, Joffre Lakes and Wells Gray Provincial Park, this British Columbia itinerary is packed with some of the province's most beautiful outdoor experiences.

01

BEFORE YOU GO

Set yourself up
for the road.

British Columbia weather can change quickly and this itinerary covers a lot of ground. A little preparation will make the trip much more enjoyable.

01

Pack Layers

Keep a light jacket handy because British Columbia weather can change quickly.

02

Wear Comfortable Hiking Shoes

Several days include hikes and forest trails, so comfortable hiking shoes are a must.

03

Carry Water + Snacks

Keep water and snacks with you, especially for hiking days and longer drives.

04

Download Offline Maps

Some routes can have limited connectivity, so download your maps before setting out.

05

Start Early

Starting early helps avoid crowds and makes the most of daylight on this drive-heavy itinerary.

06

Book Your Joffre Lakes Permit

If Joffre Lakes is part of your plan, book the required permit in advance.

Falak & Kartik Tip

Build buffer time into every day for traffic, parking and unexpected delays. This itinerary covers a lot of ground.

02

DAY 1

Fraser Valley
+ Hot Springs

We started the trip by leaving Vancouver and heading toward Fraser Valley and Harrison for an easy mix of waterfalls, lake views and a relaxing evening.

01

Bridal Veil Falls

Drive to Bridal Veil Falls and begin with a short, scenic and easy trail — a great first stop before continuing deeper into Fraser Valley.

02

Harrison Hot Springs

In the afternoon, head to Harrison Hot Springs and spend time exploring Harrison Lake and the village.

03

Harrison Hot Springs Resort & Spa

End the day relaxing at Harrison Hot Springs Resort & Spa, followed by sunset by the lake.

ROUTE Vancouver → Fraser Valley → Bridal Veil Falls → Harrison Hot Springs → Harrison Lake → Sunset

03

DAY 2

North Vancouver
+ Sea to Sky

Day 2 combines one of North Vancouver's most recognizable attractions with the scenic Sea to Sky Highway and mountain views from the gondola.

01

Capilano Suspension Bridge + Cliffwalk

Start the morning at Capilano Suspension Bridge and include the Cliffwalk while you are there.

02

Sea to Sky Highway

Drive toward Squamish along the Sea to Sky Highway. The drive itself is one of the highlights of this day.

03

Sea to Sky Gondola

Ride the gondola and explore the Panorama Trail, Sea to Sky Summit and views of Sky Pilot Mountain.

04

Sunset at the Summit

Stay for sunset from the gondola summit before wrapping up the day.

ROUTE Vancouver → Capilano → Sea to Sky Highway → Sea to Sky Gondola → Panorama Trail → Summit
Our Tip

If the weather is clear, give yourself enough time at the summit so you are not rushing through the trails before sunset.

04

DAY 3

Waterfalls
+ Coastal Sunset

This day is all about waterfalls before ending with a more relaxed coastal evening at White Rock.

01

Mamquam Falls

Start the morning with Mamquam Falls.

02

Brandywine Falls

Continue to Brandywine Falls for another dramatic waterfall stop.

03

Alexander Falls

Spend the afternoon at Alexander Falls before beginning the drive toward White Rock.

04

White Rock Pier

End the day at White Rock Pier with a sunset walk and time at the local cafés.

ROUTE Squamish → Mamquam Falls → Brandywine Falls → Alexander Falls → Vancouver → White Rock Pier

05

DAY 4

Lakes
+ Forest Trails

Day 4 mixes a lake hike, river and lake stops around Chilliwack, and an evening in the forest around Lynn Canyon.

01

Lindeman Lake

Start with the Lindeman Lake hike. Our itinerary allows around 1.5–2 hours for this stop.

02

Chilliwack River + Cultus Lake

Spend the afternoon exploring Chilliwack River and Cultus Lake.

03

Lynn Canyon Suspension Bridge

In the evening, head to North Vancouver for Lynn Canyon Suspension Bridge.

04

Twin Falls + 30 Foot Pool

While exploring Lynn Canyon, add Twin Falls and the 30 Foot Pool to your route.

ROUTE Vancouver → Chilliwack → Lindeman Lake → Chilliwack River → Cultus Lake → Lynn Canyon → Twin Falls → 30 Foot Pool

06

DAY 5

Joffre
Lakes

Day 5 is dedicated to Joffre Lakes Provincial Park, one of the biggest hiking days in this itinerary.

01

Start Early

We recommend starting around 5–6 AM and driving to Joffre Lakes Provincial Park before beginning the hike.

02

Lower Joffre Lake

The first stop is Lower Joffre Lake, around a 5-minute walk from the start.

03

Middle Joffre Lake

Continue to Middle Joffre Lake, which we marked as having some of the best views of the hike.

04

Holloway Falls

Add Holloway Falls as you continue along the trail.

05

Upper Joffre Lake

Finish at Upper Joffre Lake for the glacier view. Spend some time relaxing at the lake before descending back down.

06

Return to Vancouver

Drive back to Vancouver in the evening for dinner and rest.

ROUTE Vancouver → Joffre Lakes → Lower Lake → Middle Lake → Holloway Falls → Upper Lake → Vancouver
Our Tip

Book your Joffre Lakes permit in advance and start early. This is one of the days where planning ahead matters most.

07

DAY 6

Wells Gray
Provincial Park

The final day is also the longest driving day of the itinerary, with a 6–7 hour drive from Vancouver to Clearwater.

01

Early Start

A 5 AM start is recommended because of the long drive and the number of stops planned for the day.

02

Spahats Creek Falls

Once you reach the Wells Gray area, begin with Spahats Creek Falls.

03

Dawson Falls

Continue to Dawson Falls during the afternoon.

04

Helmcken Falls

Helmcken Falls is the highlight of the day and one of the main stops in the Wells Gray portion of the itinerary.

05

Moul Falls — Optional

If time and energy allow, add the short hike to Moul Falls.

06

Overnight Near Clearwater

Because of the distance covered on Day 6, stay overnight near Clearwater rather than driving straight back.

ROUTE Vancouver → Clearwater → Spahats Creek Falls → Dawson Falls → Helmcken Falls → Moul Falls → Overnight near Clearwater
Our Tip

Day 6 is long. An overnight stay near Clearwater makes this day much more manageable.

FINAL THOUGHTS

British Columbia gives you a little bit of everything.

British Columbia gives you a little bit of everything in one road trip — mountain drives, waterfalls, lake hikes, forest trails, hot springs and coastal sunsets.

Six days is packed, but starting early and planning the longer driving days carefully makes it possible to experience an incredible variety of landscapes.

If you love road trips and outdoor adventures, save this itinerary for your British Columbia bucket list.
