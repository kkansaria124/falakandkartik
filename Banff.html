Before You Go

A few things made a big difference during our trip. The Rockies can change quickly, and the itinerary includes long drives and plenty of walking.

Pack Layers

Mountain weather can change incredibly quickly, so having layers with you makes a huge difference.

Wear Comfortable Shoes

You'll be doing plenty of walking between viewpoints, lakes and trails.

Bring Snacks and Water

Some days involve long drives with lots of stops, so we always kept snacks and water in the car.

Download Offline Maps

Cell service can be unreliable while driving through the mountains. Download your route before leaving.

Carry a Power Bank

Between navigation, taking photos and filming videos, your phone battery disappears quickly.

Start Your Days Early

We started some days around 4 AM, and it completely changed our Banff experience.

Plan Moraine Lake transportation in advance. This is one thing we wouldn't leave until the last minute.
DAY 01

Banff Highlights

Lakes, viewpoints, waterfalls and a little time in downtown Banff.

Banff Gondola

We started our Banff adventure with one of the area's classic experiences — the Banff Gondola.

Banff Upper Hot Springs

Add this if you have extra time and want a slower experience.

Grassi Lakes

A scenic stop that gives you something different from the larger lakes later in the trip.

Johnston Canyon

We visited Lower Falls. Focusing on Lower Falls makes this stop easier to fit into a short itinerary.

Two Jack Lake and Lake Minnewanka

Two beautiful lake stops surrounded by classic Canadian Rockies scenery.

Vermilion Lakes

We saved Vermilion Lakes for sunset — the perfect way to finish the first day.

Banff Downtown

Spend some time walking around downtown Banff before heading back to your hotel.

ROUTE Banff Gondola → Grassi Lakes → Johnston Canyon → Two Jack Lake → Lake Minnewanka → Vermilion Lakes → Banff
DAY 02

Icefields Parkway and Jasper

A BIG day. Start as early as possible because the drive itself is part of the experience.

Sunwapta Falls

A great waterfall stop to stretch your legs before continuing along the route.

Columbia Icefield and Skywalk

One of the major experiences of the day. Give yourself enough time here rather than treating it as a quick viewpoint.

Tangle Creek Falls

A beautiful waterfall stop along the drive.

Medicine Lake Lookout

Continue toward Jasper and stop for another scenic stretch of the drive.

Maligne Lake Cruise and Spirit Island

One of the experiences we were most excited about. Account for the cruise time when planning the rest of the day.

Athabasca Falls and Scenic Lookouts

Finish with Athabasca Falls, Athabasca Pass Lookout, Stutfield Glacier Viewpoint and Waterfowl Lake.

ROUTE Sunwapta Falls → Columbia Icefield and Skywalk → Tangle Creek Falls → Medicine Lake → Maligne Lake and Spirit Island → Athabasca Falls → Waterfowl Lake
Falak & Kartik Tip Don't underestimate how long this day can become. Starting early makes a huge difference.
DAY 03

Hidden Gems and Scenic Spots

Scenic viewpoints, waterfalls and some of our favourite stops outside the main Banff highlights.

Banff Sign and Downtown

Grab the classic Banff sign photo and spend some extra time exploring downtown.

Peyto Lake

One of the most recognizable views in the Canadian Rockies.

Takakkaw Falls

A dramatic waterfall surrounded by an incredible mountain landscape.

Emerald Lake and Natural Bridge

Emerald Lake has a completely different feel from some of Banff's busier attractions.

Wapta Falls

Continue the waterfall theme with this memorable stop.

Golden Skybridge

We used Golden Skybridge as our lunch and fuel stop.

Crowfoot Glacier Viewpoint and Mistaya Canyon

Finish the day's sightseeing with two scenic stops before heading back.

ROUTE Banff → Peyto Lake → Takakkaw Falls → Emerald Lake → Natural Bridge → Wapta Falls → Golden Skybridge → Crowfoot Glacier → Mistaya Canyon
DAY 04

Moraine Lake and Lake Louise

We saved two of the most iconic places in the Canadian Rockies for the final day.

Moraine Lake and Rockpile Trail

We headed to Moraine Lake early and walked the Rockpile Trail for the famous view. Around sunrise, the calm water, mountains and early morning light made this one of our favourite moments of the entire trip.

Lake Louise and Big Beehive

Instead of only seeing the lake from the shoreline, we included the Big Beehive hike. Dedicate a good portion of the day if you're hiking.

Herbert Lake

A quieter lake stop to add while driving through the area.

Bow Lake

Our final lake stop and a beautiful way to wrap up the Canadian Rockies road trip.

Plan Moraine Lake transportation in advance. This isn't something we'd recommend figuring out once you arrive.

THE ONE THING WE'D DO AGAIN

Wake up early.
Every. Single. Day.

We started some mornings as early as 4 AM and, looking back, we're so glad we did. Popular locations that become extremely busy later in the day felt completely different early in the morning.

Calm lakes, soft sunrise colours, quiet viewpoints and barely anyone around. Yes, waking up at 4 AM on vacation sounds slightly crazy. But for Banff? 100% worth it.

Final Thoughts

Banff is one of those destinations that somehow looks even better in real life than it does in photos.

Between the turquoise lakes, endless mountain views, waterfalls, glaciers and scenic drives, there was something incredible around almost every corner.

Four days were packed, but starting our mornings early helped us see so much while still getting those peaceful moments that made the trip feel special.

If Banff and Jasper are on your bucket list, save this itinerary for later.

— Falak and Kartik
