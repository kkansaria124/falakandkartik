01 PREPARATION

Before You Go

A few things we recommend keeping in mind before heading to Alaska.

01

Pack Layers

Bring T-shirts, a fleece and a waterproof jacket. Alaska weather can change quickly.

02

Waterproof Shoes

Comfortable waterproof shoes make a big difference when spending long days outdoors.

03

Mosquito Repellent

This was one of the preparation items we considered especially important.

04

Sunglasses + Sunscreen

Even in cooler weather, keep both with you during day trips.

05

Small Backpack

Useful for cruises, short hikes and glacier activities.

06

Cruise Essentials

If you are taking a cruise, bring binoculars and motion-sickness medication.

07

Always Keep a Jacket Handy

Even if the day looks sunny, keep an extra layer nearby.

08

Sleep Mask

The days are extremely long, with almost no night during our trip.

09

Offline Maps + Cash

Helpful for road-trip days and areas with limited connectivity.

10

Book Activities Early

June is peak season according to our itinerary, so book major activities ahead.

FALAK & KARTIK TIP

Alaska days can feel almost endless in summer. Use the long daylight to your advantage, but still build in enough rest for early starts and full activity days.

FINAL THOUGHTS

Alaska was worth every mile.

Alaska was one of those trips where the experiences felt just as memorable as the landscapes.

From glacier cruises and scenic road trips to wildlife, helicopter views and actually walking on a glacier, every day gave us something completely different.

It is a packed 5-day itinerary, but if you want an adventure-focused Alaska trip and are comfortable with early starts and full days, this route captures a lot in a short amount of time.

Save this guide for your Alaska trip — and book the major activities early if you are travelling during peak season.
